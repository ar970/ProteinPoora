    Questions, answered

    What people ask before the first batch.

    Where does the protein actually come from?

    From the snack itself, not a coating. The chakli are built on pea protein, the pudina bhujia on soy protein isolate, and the peanuts bring their own. That is 9 g a pack in the chakli, 14 g in the bhujia and 18 g in the Korean BBQ peanuts.

    Are they fried?

    Never deep fried. The bhujia and the chakli are baked; the peanuts are roasted. There is no palm oil in any of the five.

    Are they vegetarian?

    All five are 100% vegetarian. Cheddar Cheese Chakli contains milk solids, so it is not vegan — the full ingredient list for every snack is on its own product page.

    What about allergens?

    Korean BBQ Peanuts contain peanuts and soya. Pudina Bhujia contains soy, and is made in a facility that also handles nuts, seeds and milk-based products. Cheddar Cheese Chakli contains milk.

    Every product page carries that snack's own ingredients and allergen statement. Read it there before you order — it is the one that counts.

    Can I pick my own flavours?

    Yes — build your own box. Any 5 packs or more, ₹90 each, in whatever mix you like: five of one, one of everything, up to 20 of any single flavour.

    Five is the floor, not the ceiling. Below five we cannot send a box, so a single pack on its own is not something we sell — take a combo instead if you only want one or two.

    How big is a pack?

    50 g for the chakli, 60 g for the bhujia, 65 g for the peanuts. One pack is one sitting, and the protein figures on this page are for the whole pack rather than a serving of it.

    How should I store them?

    Somewhere cool and dry, out of direct sunlight. Once a pack is open, finish it — which has not been a problem so far.

    When do I pay?

    At checkout, securely through Razorpay — card, UPI, net banking or a wallet. Nothing is taken before you place the order, and nothing after.

    When will my order arrive?

    We deliver across Bengaluru and email you when your order is on its way. If anything is going to take longer than usual, we will tell you rather than leave you guessing.

    Where do you deliver?

    Bengaluru only, for now. The checkout accepts 560xxx PIN codes and nothing else, so you will know before you pay rather than after. We would rather deliver properly in one city than badly in twenty — more cities as soon as we can do them justice.

    What does delivery cost?

    Free on orders of ₹450 or more. Below that it is ₹100, added at the checkout and shown to you before you pay — never after.

    The ₹450 is counted on the snacks, not on the delivery charge. Five packs at ₹90 come to exactly ₹450, so every box you can build ships free — and so does the Poora Family Pack, which is the same ₹450.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    Any time before it goes out. Call or WhatsApp 93113 49922, or email archit@ug30.mesaschool.co with the name and mobile number you ordered with. A cancelled order is refunded to whatever you paid from.
