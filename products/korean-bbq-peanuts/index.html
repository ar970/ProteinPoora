Product description

Whole peanuts roasted in sunflower oil, never deep fried, then coated in a Korean barbecue seasoning built on chilli, ginger, garlic and soy. Smoky and sweet first, hot underneath.

A 65 g pack carries 18 g of protein and 6 g of dietary fibre. Serving size 40 g.

Ingredients

Peanuts, edible vegetable oil (sunflower oil).

Seasoning: sugar, common salt, maltodextrin, spices and spice extract (chilli, ginger), dehydrated vegetable powder and extract (garlic, onion), acidity regulator (INS 262(ii), INS 270, INS 330), hydrolysed vegetable protein (soya base), soya sauce powder, anti-caking agent (INS 551, INS 341(iii)), flavour enhancers (INS 627, INS 631), paprika extract (INS 160c).

Natural and nature-identical flavouring substances (barbecue, smoke, garlic).

Allergens: contains peanuts and soya.
