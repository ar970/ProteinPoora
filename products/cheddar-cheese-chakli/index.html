Product description

The chakli you grew up on, rebuilt. Pea protein and jowar flour instead of plain maida, never deep fried, with no palm oil. Seasoned with real cheese powder and milk solids, so it tastes like cheddar rather than cheese flavouring.

Serving size 30 g, about 1.7 serves in a 50 g pack.

Ingredients

Pea protein, jowar flour, white lentil flour, tapioca starch, rice flour, refined vegetable oil (rice bran oil), iodised salt.

Seasoning: cheese powder, maltodextrin, milk solids, iodised salt, sugar, acidity regulator (INS 296, INS 330), flavour enhancer (INS 631, INS 627), anti-caking agents (INS 551, INS 341(iii)).

Contains added natural and nature-identical flavouring substances.

Allergen: contains milk.
