Product description

The chakli you grew up on, rebuilt. Pea protein and jowar flour instead of plain maida, never deep fried, with no palm oil. Sweet chilli seasoning with tamarind and Thai pepper, so it lands sweet, then hot, then tangy.

Serving size 30 g, about 1.7 serves in a 50 g pack.

Ingredients

Pea protein, jowar flour, white lentil flour, tapioca starch, rice flour, refined vegetable oil (rice bran oil), iodised salt.

Seasoning: spices and condiments (Thai pepper powder, red chilli, black pepper, spray dried tamarind powder, dehydrated garlic powder), spray dried apple vinegar powder, maltose, dextrose, sugar, acidifying agent (INS 330, INS 296), flavour enhancers (INS 627, INS 631), anti-caking agent (INS 170(i)).

Contains natural and nature-identical flavouring substances: red chilli oil.
